Intent & Goal
A peer-to-peer marketplace that allows users to trade energy they generate. The platform leverages the pricing spread between government feed-in tariffs and retail energy rates, ensuring trades are financially beneficial for both parties. It features an integrated wallet system to make transactions seamless.
Audience & Roles
Anyone connected to the public grid. Users can act as 'Producers' (creating listings to sell surplus energy) or 'Consumers' (browsing and buying energy). All authenticated users have access to both buying and selling features.
Core Flows
These flows must work end-to-end:
User signs up and connects their profile (identifying as a potential producer or consumer).
Users can manage an in-app balance: topping it up manually, viewing funds from energy sales, and withdrawing funds to an external account.
Producer creates an energy listing, specifying the amount and setting a custom rate that sits profitably between the government buy and sell rates.
Consumer browses the open marketplace, filtering available energy listings by price and volume.
Consumer claims a listing and pays for it using their in-app balance, instantly transferring the funds to the seller's balance.
Users view their transaction history, active listings, and energy purchased/sold.
